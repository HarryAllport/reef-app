Livestock in the tank

Add what you actually keep — corals, clams, fish and inverts — and how big it is. Targets refine to the overlap of their published care ranges (a butterflyfish caps nitrate, a Linckia starfish demands cleaner water still), and calcifiers drive the demand estimate until your log can measure the real thing.

Maintenance reminders

A saved page can’t send email on its own, so reminders go through your calendar: this downloads repeating water change, testing and dosing events with alerts addressed to the email above. Salt & water changes
Doing a water change today? Plan it here first. ICP analysis

Clear a range of entries
Uptake and dosing rates are worked out from your log, so a spell that no longer reflects the tank — before a big stocking change, a period the skimmer was off, a run of readings from a dud test kit — drags the averages with it. Removing that stretch leaves the rest intact.
ICP reports
Lab analyses are kept here rather than in the test log, because a report is a snapshot of water drawn days before the results land — mixing the two would put the wrong dates on your trends.
Feeding
What the animals in this tank actually eat, drawn from the livestock you have added. Corals are not all photosynthetic and fish are not all flake-eaters — the ones that need more than the tank provides are the ones that quietly starve.

Data
Your settings, log and reports are stored by this browser on this device — nothing leaves it, and nothing is on a server. Two things clear that store, and neither is the app: deleting the home screen icon on an iPhone or iPad throws away everything that icon held, and clearing website data does the same. Take a backup before you remove or re-add the icon, and keep one anyway.
