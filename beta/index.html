ICP reports
Lab analyses are kept here rather than in the test log, because a report is a snapshot of water drawn days before the results land — mixing the two would put the wrong dates on your trends.
Feeding
What the animals in this tank actually eat, drawn from the livestock you have added. Corals are not all photosynthetic and fish are not all flake-eaters — the ones that need more than the tank provides are the ones that quietly starve.
What's on your shelf
Tick everything you have to hand. Corrections are worked out from these and nothing else. The strength figures come from the labels — change any that your bottle disagrees with.
What you feed
Tick what you actually use. Foods are not dosed like supplements — they have a routine rather than a strength — so this drives the feeding reminders rather than the chemistry. Each group opens on its own.

Daily limits
Corrections larger than these get split across several days. The water change ceiling decides the order of the plan: a change up to that size is done first and the doses worked out from the water afterwards, while anything larger means supplementing first to close the gap and a smaller change after.
